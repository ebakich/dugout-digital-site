What we read

From the stadium radar to the bat sensor.

Every instrument measures something a little differently. We know what each one is good at, where it is unreliable, and how to put them side by side without mixing them up.

Stadium radar and opticalTrackMan · Hawk-Eye

The gold standard for ball flight: exit velocity, launch angle, direction, distance, pitch velocity, spin and movement on every pitch and every ball in play, plus bat tracking when the system runs in game mode. We read the full export, including the columns most people never open.

Portable radar and opticalPortable TrackMan · Rapsodo · Pocket Radar · Stalker

The cage, the bullpen and the travel-ball field. Smaller samples and different settings, so every number is labeled with the device and the setting it came from before it is compared to anything.

Bat and ball sensorsBlast · Diamond Kinetics · HitTrax · smart balls

Bat speed, swing plane, time to contact, spin. Some readings are trustworthy and some are not, and we have audited them against radar on the same swing. We tell you which is which.

Biomechanics labsMarkerless motion capture · force plates

The body behind the number: how the legs load, how the hips and shoulders separate, how force moves into the bat. We translate the lab report into the one or two movement patterns that explain the ball-flight numbers.

CSV · XLSX · JSON · a PDF report · a screenshot of the app. Send what you have and we will tell you what it can and cannot say.
