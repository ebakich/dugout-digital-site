Your numbers

Six numbers every parent should know.

0 mph

Exit velocity

How hard the ball leaves the bat. Harder is better.

0°

Launch angle

How high it leaves. 10 to 30 is where doubles live.

0 mph

Bat speed

How fast the barrel moves. More speed, more exit velo.

0 rpm

Spin rate

How many times the pitch turns per minute. More spin, more ride.

0 s

Sixty-yard dash

Speed, timed to the hundredth. Under 7.0 turns heads.

0 mph

Arm strength

How hard you throw, from the mound or across the infield.
