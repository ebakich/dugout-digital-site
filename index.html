Any data. Any device. Plain English.

What we read

Any file from any of these.

From the radar bolted to a college stadium to the sensor on a bat knob. If it produced a file, we can translate it.

Stadium radar and optical

Stadium TrackMan and Hawk-Eye exports. Every pitch, every ball in play, bat tracking when it ran.

Portable radar

Portable TrackMan, Rapsodo, Pocket Radar, Stalker. The cage, the bullpen, the travel-ball field on a Saturday.

Bat and ball sensors

Blast, Diamond Kinetics, HitTrax, smart balls. Bat speed, swing plane, contact. We also say which readings to trust.

Biomechanics labs

Markerless motion capture and force plates. The body behind the number, in words a parent can use.

How it works

Capture anywhere. Translate once. Train all year.

1

Capture

At a camp, a showcase, a tournament, a lesson, or your own cage. Any device that writes a file.

2

Send the file

Email it, or the event sends it for everyone at once. We log the instrument, the setting and the date, because a number without them is a guess.

3

We translate

A coach reads every evaluation before it ships. Strength, growth, benchmark, plan. Plain English, small samples labeled.

4

Train and re-test

The plan lives in the app. The next capture shows what moved. Opt in, and coaches can find the verified numbers on their own.

What we will never do

  • Sell a ranking. We publish measurements, the conditions they were measured in, and leaderboards families can see for themselves.
  • Make the call for a college. We do no outreach and no introductions. Coaches search the database on their own; players decide what they see.
  • Copy data from other services. Every number was captured on a known instrument on a known day, or added by the family and labeled as theirs.
  • Sell to a family directly or hold a family's money. Camps, teams, events, programs and TBC23 are our customers.
  • Pitch fear. The product is the feeling of knowing exactly where you stand and what to do about it.
