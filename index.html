Player Portal

Every verified number, searchable the way you search for a house.

  • Every evaluation feeds the portal. A player who gets a card at your event has his verified numbers in it the same day, if his family opts in.
  • College coaches filter like Zillow. State, grad year, position, height, weight, exit velocity, sixty, fastball velocity, and every other number on the card.
  • Leaderboards, not rankings. By event or by age group. Sorting by a number is the coach's choice, never ours.
  • Trust built in. Every number carries the event, the instrument and the date. Players choose what is visible and can change it any time.
