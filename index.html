What we build

Four pieces. One record of the player.

The Evaluation Engine

From a test battery to a pro-style card

Exit velocity, launch, bat speed, pitch data, speed and arm, scored against published thresholds and written up the way a college staff writes up its own players. Small samples are shown as small samples. The player sees his own numbers, never a rank.

The Camp Kit

A data standard any TrackMan camp can run

Exact roster names, a Data Captain, tagged pitches, tracked swings, instrument and setting logged, lineups before the first pitch, a QA gate. Pass the gate and every camper leaves with a card in the host camp's brand. No standard, no card, no charge.

The Player App

From evaluation to plan to game journal

The card becomes a strength, a growth target and a drill series with every drill on video, a game-day tap journal, a mental-game module and a coach-to-coach email the family sends itself. Built for a college program first, then handed to campers.

The Team Report

Twelve players, one coach-facing read

A travel team or a tournament field measured on the same battery, with a team report the coach can plan a season from and an individual card for every player. Same engine, same thresholds, same honesty about sample size.

How a partner camp works

Capture it right once. Train from it all year.

1

Capture

Your camp runs the data standard on your instruments. Every swing and every pitch is measured the same way, so a card from one weekend means the same thing as a card from the next.

2

Verify

A coach reviews every evaluation before it ships. Anything that fails the standard is marked, not inflated, and never billed.

3

Deliver

Your camp delivers the card to the family, in your brand, at your price. We invoice you per player. We never sell to the family and never hold their money.

4

Train

The card opens in the player app: the strength to keep, the one thing to build, the drills to build it, and the next camp to prove it.

What we will never do

  • Rate, rank or place a player. We publish measurements and the conditions they were measured in.
  • Send a player's information to a college. The family owns the card and decides who sees it.
  • Copy data from other services. Every number was captured at a camp that runs our standard.
  • Sell to a family, hold a family's money, or give anything away to a prospect. Camps and organizations are our customers.
  • Pitch a family on fear. The product is the feeling of having given yourself the best chance.
Who we are

Built in a dugout. Tested by the players it is for.

Dugout Digital is a family-owned software and operations company in Clemson, South Carolina. It runs the technology and product work behind TBC23, a college-coach-run camp and player-development business, and owns the evaluation engine, the camp kit, the player app and the team report described here. The systems were built for a college program first: an evaluation engine, a plan generator that writes activation and drill series from the diagnosis, a game-day journal and a mental-game module.

The product testers are the junior-high and high-school players it is for. The company sells to camps and organizations; families buy from their camp or from TBC23.
